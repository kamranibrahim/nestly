Common help

  • Create a nest — Sign up, then Start nest and share your invite code.
  • Join a nest — Enter the invite code from a family member.
  • Offline — Edits work without signal; they sync when you’re back online.
  • Export or delete — In the app: Nest → Privacy.
  • Notifications — Enable alerts in system settings for bill and care reminders.
