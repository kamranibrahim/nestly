# Casaio Plus — product one-pager (draft)

**Status:** Draft only for next-quarter planning. **No paywall ships in the free soft-launch cycle.**

## Problem
Families that already run Casaio as their weekly OS want optional depth — without forcing a paywall on the core nest.

## Who
Multi-member nests (2+) that use Casaio ≥3 days/week for tasks, calendar, shopping, and care/school.

## Positioning
Casaio stays free for the family OS. **Plus** is an optional upgrade for households that want richer automation and history — never a lock on invite, sync, or today’s essentials.

## Candidate Plus value (prioritize later)
1. **Longer history & exports** — deeper timeline retention; richer CSV/PDF exports  
2. **Automation helpers** — smarter restock / meal→list suggestions; digest customization  
3. **Widgets & surfaces** — Android widget parity; lock-screen / Watch stretch  
4. **Nest admin** — member activity insights; role templates; leave/transfer nest polish  
5. **Peace of mind** — priority support; optional encrypted vault backup packs  

Explicitly **not** Plus (remain free): create/join nest, sync, core modules, invite, emergency card, basic export, Today widget v1.

## Pricing sketch (TBD)
- Soft launch: free forever for core  
- Plus: monthly / annual family plan (one subscription covers the nest)  
- No ads; no dark-pattern trial gates on invite

## Success metrics (when we build)
- Plus attach rate among nests with 2+ members and age ≥14d  
- Churn after 30d; support tickets about “paywall blocking basics” = 0  
- Core free Day-7 open remains healthy

## Open questions
- Domain / branded email before Plus marketing?  
- Social auth before paid?  
- Unpause web companion under Plus or free?

## Decision
File this draft. Revisit after free soft launch metrics (Week 16 retro). Do **not** add IAP, paywall screens, or feature flags that hide core Casaio.
